ordinary British office life; fits on a phone; hurts nobody's feelings. Cut
anything that fails one of those, or that everyone in your office would answer
the same way. Strike through or delete; I'll build the pack from what's left.

## Notes for the cut

- **33 and 37** could split by age more than by taste. That might be the fun of
  it, or it might make the top answer predictable in your office.
- **24 and 30** are jokier. One joke option per prompt keeps it light, but if
  half the room picks the joke the survey says nothing.
- **4 and 7** are the most likely to land on one obvious answer (beef, fish).
  Worth checking against your gut before keeping them.
